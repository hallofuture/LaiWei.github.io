Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy centers on making complex concepts accessible through hands-on, project-based learning. I believe that students learn best when they can connect theoretical concepts to real-world applications, particularly in interdisciplinary fields like materials informatics and AI.

I emphasize active learning through practical projects, where students gain experience with cutting-edge tools and techniques while developing problem-solving skills. By integrating service-learning components, I help students understand how their technical skills can address meaningful challenges in science and society.
