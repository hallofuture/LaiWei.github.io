About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at the University of South Carolina-Columbia, with a focus on Crystal Structure Prediction and Deep Learning-Based Generative Materials Design. My dissertation explores cutting-edge approaches to materials informatics, combining artificial intelligence with computational materials science.

I specialize in developing machine learning and deep learning techniques for crystal structure prediction, and generative materials design. My research contributes to advancing computational methods for materials discovery and has applications in designing new materials with desired properties.
